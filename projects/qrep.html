01 / CONTEXT

The product.

Qrep is a support platform designed around the day-to-day work of customer service teams. The interfaces cover conversations, ticket handling, shifts, presence checks, and reporting.

02 / MY WORK

What I designed.

  • Support conversations and ticket handling
  • Shift schedules and staff presence checks
  • Ticket and employee performance reports
  • Web and mobile layouts, plus interface components

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

The thinking
in the interface.

01

Keep work in context

Tickets, conversations, and the working schedule are parts of one support workflow. The navigation gives each a clear place without losing the wider workspace.

02

Design for more than one role

An agent needs the next customer issue. A manager needs team status and performance. The layouts give these responsibilities distinct views.

03

Account for the working environment

Display themes, notification sounds, and text preferences are part of the interface. These controls let a support workspace fit different working preferences.

04 / DELIVERY

Where it stands.

Designed web and mobile interfaces covering the support workflow, with dedicated pages for development handoff.
