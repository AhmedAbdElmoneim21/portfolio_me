01 / CONTEXT

The product.

NewAvenue is a mobile platform for real estate agents to share inventory and client requests, discover opportunities, and talk to one another. I was the sole freelance UI/UX designer. The design also includes an internal conflict team for reviewing duplicate listings and disputes.

02 / MY WORK

What I designed.

  • Property and request feed with search and filters
  • Step-by-step inventory and client request creation
  • Agent profiles, comments, and private conversations
  • Conflict reporting, evidence requests, and resolution states

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

The thinking
in the interface.

01

Bring supply and demand together

Inventory and client requests sit in one feed with distinct states, so agents can move between available properties and what a client needs.

02

Break listing creation into clear steps

Property information, details, location, media, and description are separated into a guided flow.

03

Give disputes a defined route

Agents can report a conflict from a conversation. Dedicated agent and conflict-team views handle evidence requests, review, and resolution.
