01 / CONTEXT

The product.

OFUQ is a travel eSIM app. I designed package discovery, checkout, installation, and SIM management flows. The cover shows an early visual direction under the working name Shreeha; the released product is named OFUQ.

02 / MY WORK

What I designed.

  • Destination browsing and package duration selection
  • Checkout and order confirmation
  • QR, manual, and direct installation guidance
  • Account settings and SIM management

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

The thinking
in the interface.

01

Make setup part of the product

Installation guidance offers different routes for setting up the eSIM, with the relevant instructions available from the SIM details.

02

Show important states

The designs include successful purchase, payment failure, offline, and issuance states, so the flow accounts for more than the ideal path.

04 / DELIVERY

Where it stands.

OFUQ is available on the App Store and Google Play under the name أفق: شرائح انترنت لا محدود.
