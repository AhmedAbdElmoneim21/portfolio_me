01 / CONTEXT

The product.

A football app with match views, news, team and player statistics, and favourites. I designed light and dark interface themes and the onboarding choices that shape the experience.

02 / MY WORK

What I designed.

  • Favourite clubs and leagues during onboarding
  • Match schedules, live states, and results
  • Team and player information and statistics
  • News browsing, search, and light and dark themes

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

The thinking
in the interface.

01

Let fans choose what matters

Favourite teams and leagues give the match list a personal entry point, alongside the complete competition view.

02

Use the match state as information

Live, upcoming, and completed matches have distinct states, so time and score are understood together.

Where it stands.

Designed the mobile interface, reusable elements, and theme variations.
